// What it is

Big work doesn't fit in one chat.

A migration, a new product area, a rewrite. Agents can do the work, but the plan, the open decisions and the blockers end up scattered across sessions. MILOOPS keeps them in one mission you can see.

01 · Plan

A mission, not a prompt

A mission holds the destination, decisions so far, what's not yet specified and what's out of scope. Work is split into tickets, and each one knows what it's waiting on.

02 · Delegate

Agents take what's unblocked

Agents pick up tasks that are ready, one at a time, and write down what they did when they finish.

03 · Decide

You keep the calls that matter

Decision tickets ask you directly. An agent can't close one without your answer, and every answer lands in the mission's decisions.

// Meet MILO

Your operator.

Mission Intelligence & Logistics Operator

MILO sets you up, keeps an eye on every agent and tells you how the mission is going. When an agent needs a decision, MILO brings it to you and waits for your answer.

Built for operators, not spectators.

  • Missions with decision tickets

    Big goals broken into clear steps. Every decision you make is written down on the mission.

// FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something missing? Open an issue on GitHub.

What is MILOOPS?

A command center for planning and finishing big projects with AI coding agents. It runs on your computer and shows every mission, task, agent and open question in one window.

Who is MILO?

MILO, the Mission Intelligence & Logistics Operator, is the voice of the command center. It reports status, relays agents' questions to you and briefs you on the mission. It never answers on your behalf.

Which agents does it work with?

Any AI coding agent that connects through MCP. Claude Code and Codex have first-class support.

Does my data leave my machine?

MILOOPS runs on your computer and keeps your plans in your project folder. Your agents still use their own AI services.

Do I need GitHub or Linear?

No. Plans are saved in your project by default, with no account. GitHub and Linear sync is coming as an option.

Can an agent make a decision for me?

No. A decision can't be closed until you've answered it. The agent drafts a summary, and you confirm it.

How is spend tracked?

Each agent reports what it uses. You see the cost per agent and for the whole mission, always on screen.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything that runs on your machine is free and open source. A hosted version for teams will be a paid product later.

Technical details
  • Install: npm i -g miloops, then run miloops in your project folder. Or run it once with npx miloops. Requires Node.js 20 or newer.
  • Local server: starts on localhost:7420 (the next free port if that one is busy) and opens your browser. Flags: --port, --dir for the data folder, --no-open to skip the browser.
  • Agents: connect through MCP (Model Context Protocol), so any MCP-capable coding agent works. Claude Code and Codex have first-class support with ready-made agent packs.
  • Data: missions and tickets are markdown files in .miloops/ inside your project. Nothing is sent to MILOOPS servers.
