How a mission runs

From an idea to verified work.

Four steps, in one command center that runs next to your code.

01

Describe the mission

Give it a title, where it should end up and any notes. MILO charts it into tickets with dependencies, so the order of work is clear before anyone starts.

  • Research, decisions and build work in one map
  • Plan only, or plan and execute
  • Plain files in your repo, or GitHub Issues and Linear
02

Specialists take what's ready

Agents claim unblocked tickets and work in parallel. You see who is on what, live, with tokens and cost per agent.

  • Start from your agent's chat with /milo
  • A quiet agent's claim expires and the work returns to the queue
  • Spend per session and for the whole mission
03

You make the calls that matter

When an agent needs your judgment, it asks in one inbox with suggested answers. Reply in a click, or from your agent's chat with /milo inbox.

  • Every question from every agent in one place
  • Suggested answers, or your own words
  • No agent can make the decision for you
04

Done means verified, and on the record

Your checks run before a ticket closes, a different agent reviews the change, and every decision is written down with its reasoning.

  • Tests, lint and typecheck from your repo
  • Independent review, plus your approval when you want it
  • A searchable decision log you can export

Questions

Questions, answered.

What is MILOOPS?
A command center for planning and finishing big projects with AI coding agents. It runs on your computer and shows every mission, ticket, agent and open question in one window.
Who is MILO?
MILO, the Mission Intelligence & Logistics Operator, is your sidekick in the command center. It hands out work, relays agents' questions to you and briefs you on the mission. It never answers on your behalf.
Which agents does it work with?
Any AI coding agent that connects through MCP. Claude Code and Codex have first-class support: one command connects them and adds the /milo command to their chat.
Can I stay in my agent's chat?
Yes. /milo new turns an idea into a mission, /milo resume picks up the work, /milo status tells you where things stand, and /milo inbox lets you answer waiting questions in the chat. The command center shows it all live.
Does my data leave my machine?
MILOOPS keeps your plans in your project folder. If you connect GitHub or Linear, your computer talks to them directly and your keys stay on your computer. Your agents still use their own AI services.
Can an agent make a decision for me?
No. A decision can't be closed until you've answered it. The agent drafts a summary, you confirm it, and it lands in the decision log.
Is it free?
Yes. MILOOPS is free to use and runs on your computer.
How do I install it?
Run npx miloops in your project folder (Node.js 20 or newer). Then connect your agent with npx miloops connect claude or npx miloops connect codex. Pick Demo in setup to look around a ready-made mission first.
